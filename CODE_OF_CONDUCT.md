# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any public space where the project is represented.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainer through a private GitHub Security Advisory on this repository, or by contacting the maintainer named on the repository profile. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainer will respond with the steps they will take, which may include a warning, a temporary restriction, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code may face consequences set by the project's other maintainers.
